# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Operational Excellence Analysis

## 📌 Business Overview
An end-to-end exploratory analysis of global e-commerce transaction data across 5 international markets. The objective was to uncover hidden operational bottlenecks, analyze customer purchasing behavior through RFM segmentation, and identify key drivers of revenue leakage across desktop vs. mobile checkouts and international markets.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Methods
* **Tools & Technologies:** SQL, MS Excel / Google Sheets, Statistical Analysis
* **Analytical Techniques:** RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Segmentation, Funnel Analysis, Cohort Retention Analysis

## 📈 Key Insights & Business Impact
* **Checkout Friction:** Discovered a massive **56% failure rate during Desktop checkout**, pointing directly to UX/UI friction in the payment gateway.
* **Regional Logistics & Returns:** Pinpointed critical operational issues in international markets:
  * **USA:** Unusually high return rate (**73%** in the *Home* category).
  * **Germany:** High order cancellation rate (**38%**).
* **Retention Deficit:** Revealed a **0% customer retention rate**, emphasizing an over-reliance on one-off acquisitions without active lifecycle marketing.
* **Strategic Impact:** Recommended automated email sequences for post-purchase onboarding and a dedicated "White-Glove" delivery service for the high-AOV (Average Order Value) US premium segment.
